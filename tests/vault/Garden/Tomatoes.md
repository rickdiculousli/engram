# Tomatoes
North bed, six plants, staked.

## Varieties
- Sungold (cherry, the sweetest)
- Brandywine (late, splits after rain)
- San Marzano for sauce

## Problems
Blossom end rot on the San Marzanos in August; more even watering fixed it. See [[Soil]].
